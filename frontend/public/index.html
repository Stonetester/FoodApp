Welcome to Modo Gusto!

Your personal food management app for recipes, pantry tracking, and meal planning.

Swipe or tap the arrows to learn the basics.

📖

Recipes

Add recipes manually, import from a URL, scan a QR code, or snap a photo of a recipe page. Tap the + button at the bottom of the screen to quickly add a recipe or scan a pantry item from anywhere in the app.

🧀

Pantry

Track your ingredients with barcode scanning. Point your camera at a barcode to auto-fill product info and nutrition. You can also scan a nutrition label photo.

📅

Meal Planning

Drag recipes onto your calendar to plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for the week ahead.

⭐

Discover & Review

Find other users' recipes, copy them to your collection, and leave star ratings and reviews.

🙋

Social & Friends

Add friends, share recipes via QR code or link, and see what others are cooking.

✅

You're All Set!

Tap the ? button in the top bar anytime to revisit this guide. Happy cooking!
